Bhosari: Pune Collector says names of 58 voters with changed addresses included in electoral roll

Pune Collector Jitendra Dudi says a person submitted all the Form 8 applications that changed the addresses of 60 Bhosari voters, and a case has been lodged against that person at Nigdi police station.

Published 15 Nov 2026, 12:42 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

The names of 58 voters of the Bhosari assembly constituency, whose addresses were changed to other constituencies without their knowledge before the 2024 Lok Sabha polls, have been included in the electoral roll and added to the voters list, Pune district election officer and Collector Jitendra Dudi said.

On November 1, a case was lodged against a person at Nigdi police station under the Representation of the People Act, 1950. According to Dudi, the person had submitted all the Form 8 applications to change the addresses, and none of the voters was aware of it.

Details of the person's background and motive are being gathered, and the office of the state chief electoral officer has been informed.

There were 60 such voters. Two of them have since passed away; the remaining 58 filled up Form 6.

The addresses had been changed to Thane, Khadakvasla, Nashik East, Kothrud, Pimpri, Khed Alandi and Ambegaon, sources in the Pune election department said. They also said an explanation has been sought from the electoral officer concerned on how the addresses were changed without field verification.

In November last year, the state chief electoral officer's office asked the Pune district election officer to probe the issue after NCP (SP) leader Ajit Gavhane complained with the names of all 60 voters. Gavhane said their names were deleted during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) after the change of address.

Maharashtra: 35,000 court employees on indefinite strike from 17 November, work may halt

A Group C association of court employees called an indefinite strike after a pay-hike proposal stayed pending for 22 months; earlier protests with black ribbons and then a mass leave brought no settlement.

Published 17 Nov 2026, 1:08 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

35,000 court employees across Maharashtra are on an indefinite strike from 17 November, called by the Group C association of the Maharashtra State Judicial Employees Federation, and court work may come to a halt.

The president and secretary of the Pune District Judicial Employees Group C Association said employees of the Pune District and Sessions Court will join the strike. They cited short staffing, a rising number of cases, work pressure, and pay that does not match the nature of the work. The employees have resolved to keep the strike going until their demands are met.

That Group C association called the strike after a proposal for a pay hike had stayed pending for 22 months. Earlier protests, working with black ribbons and then a mass leave, brought no settlement of the demands.

Lawyers say litigants may bear the brunt. With courts already carrying a large backlog, hearings may be delayed and pending cases may rise further.

Coming just as courts reopen after the long Diwali vacation, the strike may hit the filing of new suits, the registration of applications, obtaining certified copies of orders and carrying out bail orders.

A lawyer at the District and Sessions Court urged the government to settle the employees' demands at once.

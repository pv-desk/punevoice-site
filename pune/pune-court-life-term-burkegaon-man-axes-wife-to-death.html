Burkegaon man, drunk on suspicion, axes wife to death; Pune court awards life term

A Pune sessions court has given a Burkegaon man life imprisonment instead of the death penalty for killing his wife, ruling that the case did not fall under the ‘rarest of rare’ category.

Published 16 Nov 2026, 2:54 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

In a judgment delivered on November 7, 2026, a Pune sessions court sentenced a 53-year-old Burkegaon man to life imprisonment for killing his wife with an axe on July 23, 2024.

Additional Sessions Judge H M Bhosale also imposed a fine of ₹10,000, with three months of rigorous imprisonment in default.

According to the prosecution, the man repeatedly questioned his wife’s character and frequently ill-treated her under the influence of alcohol. The murder came to light when the couple’s daughter reached the house at around 12.30 pm, after her mother did not answer calls or report for work.

She found her mother lying in a pool of blood with a bloodstained axe nearby. The man was allegedly sleeping in the same room. When she woke him, he allegedly told her that he had killed her mother with the axe.

The woman was declared dead at Sassoon Hospital. The post-mortem found 16 external injuries, including wounds to her head, neck and back.

Blood on the man’s shirt matched her DNA profile. So did bloodstains on the axe, on the woman’s blouse and in samples from the scene. DNA analysis played a crucial role in the conviction.

The defence claimed the man was outside the house at the time. The court rejected this, relying on the daughter’s testimony, the forensic evidence and other circumstances. It ruled that the case did not fall under the ‘rarest of rare’ category and awarded life imprisonment instead of the death penalty.

Additional Public Prosecutor N P Konghe represented the prosecution.

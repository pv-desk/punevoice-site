PMC tax row: Dhangekar alleges over ₹500 crore loss, corporation says it followed court order

Former MLA Ravindra Dhangekar has questioned how the reported tax arrears of Gandhi Lawns in Kothrud were reduced from ₹26 crore to less than ₹1 crore; the Pune Municipal Corporation says a court order and applicable rules governed its decision.

Published 18 Nov 2026, 9:51 am IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Former MLA Ravindra Dhangekar has alleged irregularities in the tax assessment and collection department of the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) and accused officials of granting illegal concessions to defaulters, causing losses of more than ₹500 crore to Pune residents.

The irregularities he has alleged relate to the tax dues of Gandhi Lawns in Kothrud. He questioned how Gandhi Lawns' reported tax arrears of ₹26 crore were reduced to less than ₹1 crore.

The officials he accused are Deputy Commissioner (Tax Assessment) Ravi Pawar and Additional Commissioner Omprakash Divate. He demanded a high-level inquiry into cases approved since Pawar took charge and into the roles of both officials.

The PMC has rejected the claims of wrongdoing. It says its decision followed a court order and applicable rules.

In its clarification, the PMC said the annual rateable value (ARV) of Gandhi Lawns, initially fixed at ₹23.86 lakh in 2000, was reduced to ₹12.27 lakh after an objection hearing.

The property owner appealed to the Small Causes Court in Pune. In 2007 the court set aside the assessment and sent the matter back to the corporation for a fresh hearing and decision.

Laxmi Road · Civic

Massive Laxmi Road encroachment drive halted after Fatechand Ranka, traders intervene

A massive anti-encroachment drive on Pune's Laxmi Road was halted after the Laxmi Road Traders' Association and veteran trader leader Fatechand Ranka intervened, with civic teams pausing the crackdown.

Nearly all establishments on the commercial stretch pulled down their shutters during the drive, which tore down extended signboards, metal sheds and shopfront projections along the road.

Along the closed shops on both sides of the busy market street lay dismantled signage, torn banners and damaged storefront structures.

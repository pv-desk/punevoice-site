Pune: NET-SET, PhD holders on indefinite sit-in over 5,012 vacant assistant professor posts

The NET-SET, PhD Holders Struggle Committee, Maharashtra State, claimed that universities have not prepared the score sheets needed to recruit assistant professors for aided colleges, and alleged that appointing professors on a Clock Hour Basis violates University Grants Commission guidelines.

Published 16 Nov 2026, 8:19 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

NET-SET, PhD holders began an indefinite sit-in in Pune on Monday, outside the office of the Director of Higher Education on Senapati Bapat Road, over 5,012 assistant professor posts that are still vacant.

The NET-SET, PhD Holders Struggle Committee, Maharashtra State, alleged that nine months after the state government issued a Government Resolution (GR) to recruit 5,012 assistant professors in aided colleges across the state, not a single appointment has been made. It claimed that universities have not prepared even the score sheets needed to start the recruitment, bringing the process to a standstill.

The committee also alleged that appointing professors on a Clock Hour Basis (CHB) violates University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines. Its state coordinator said that CHB teachers, appointed for nine months on paper, often receive an honorarium for barely six months, as it is stopped during the Diwali vacation, the summer vacation and the examination period.

The state coordinator said a petition challenging the CHB system, filed before the Aurangabad Bench in 2023, is awaiting a decision.

The committee demands equal pay for equal work and an end to the CHB system. It also demands the recruitment completed immediately and transparently, and a clear GR on assessing professors' workload under NEP 2020. It says such a GR would help protect nearly 33,000 aided professor posts across Maharashtra.

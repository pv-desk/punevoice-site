Chandni Chowk · Accidents and fires

Two daily wage workers killed as motorcycle hits parked truck near Chandni Chowk, Pune

Two daily wage workers died when their motorcycle hit a parked container truck, and a case of causing death by negligence has been registered against a truck driver.

Published 13 Nov 2026, 10:32 am IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Two daily wage workers, aged 26 and 20, were killed in the early hours of Monday when their motorcycle hit a parked container truck on the Katraj-Dehu Road bypass near Chandni Chowk in Pune. Police sub-inspector Sarang Thakare of Bavdhan police gave the details.

The two lived in Kalewadi and hailed from Beed district. On Sunday night they had set out on the motorcycle for Chikkodi in Karnataka to meet the rider's parents. The rider failed to notice the stationary container truck and rammed into it; both were killed on the spot.

Police found that the truck driver had parked the vehicle without switching on its indicators. After the accident, he fled the scene instead of helping the victims. A case of causing death by negligence has been registered against the driver.

Major fire at plastic factory in Mhetrewasti, Pimpri-Chinchwad

A major fire broke out at around 3:45 pm at a plastic injection manufacturing company in the Chinchacha Mala area of Mhetrewasti, Pimpri-Chinchwad.

A large quantity of materials inside the factory is said to have been damaged. The cause of the fire is yet to be established.

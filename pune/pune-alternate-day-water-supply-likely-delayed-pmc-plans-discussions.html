Pune water supply · Civic

Pune’s shift to alternate-day water supply after Diwali likely delayed as PMC plans further discussions

Pune’s water quota is 13 TMC, against the 21 TMC that the Pune Municipal Corporation initially demanded for the current year.

Published 17 Nov 2026, 10:42 am IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Pune’s shift to alternate-day water supply after Diwali is likely to be delayed, as the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) plans to seek further discussions with the Irrigation Department over the city’s allocated water quota of 13 TMC.

The PMC’s group leaders from all political parties were to meet on November 16 to discuss the proposed cuts, but the meeting was postponed. The matter is expected to be taken up at the next meeting on November 17.

A decision will be taken after discussions involving the Irrigation Department, PMC officials and representatives of all political parties.

Mayor Manjusha Nagpure said the existing 13 TMC allocation was inadequate and that further water cuts would require detailed discussions with the Irrigation Department and political representatives.

The PMC had initially demanded 21 TMC for the current year. The Irrigation Department sanctioned 11.33 TMC and, following objections from citizens, allocated an additional 1.70 TMC, taking the total quota to 13 TMC.

The Khadakwasla dam system held 21.74 TMC on November 16, 2026, against 27.78 TMC on the same date in 2025, a deficit of 6.04 TMC.

The existing 10% cut continues: the PMC has clarified that water supply will remain suspended across the city every Thursday, and citizens have been advised to plan their water consumption accordingly.

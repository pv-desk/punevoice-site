CNG and PNG prices · Business

Pune: CNG at ₹99 a kg from midnight, household PNG prices raised too

Pune's CNG price has risen five times in a year and household PNG's twice, a total rise of ₹8.25 for CNG against ₹2.35 for PNG.

Published 12 Nov 2026, 10:33 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

MNGL India Limited has raised CNG prices by ₹1.25 a kg and household PNG prices by ₹1.50, taking CNG to ₹99 a kg in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad from midnight.

The new rates apply from midnight. CNG is ₹99.00 a kg in Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Chakan, Talegaon and Hinjewadi. Household piped gas (PNG) in the Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad municipal corporation areas will cost ₹53.00 per SCM.

CNG prices in Pune have gone up five times in the past year, and household PNG prices twice. CNG was ₹90.75 a kg in October 2025 and has risen in stages since May 2026 to ₹99. That is a total rise of ₹8.25 in the year, against ₹2.35 for PNG.

MNGL said it had to raise prices because imported natural gas (RLNG) now makes up a larger share of its total gas supply, which has raised its gas purchase and import costs.

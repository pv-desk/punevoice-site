Pune voter list scam: addresses of over 15,000 voters allegedly altered without consent

A non-cognizable offence has been lodged at Nigdi police station against four suspects accused of using four mobile phones to submit Form 8 applications fraudulently, and factual reports have been sought from election officers in eight constituencies.

Published 16 Nov 2026, 12:27 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Acting on a complaint, the State Chief Electoral Officer has ordered an inquiry into the alleged alteration of addresses of over 15,000 voters in Pune, and their shifting out of their constituencies without consent.

Ajit Gavhane, a leader of the NCP (Sharadchandra Pawar), filed a complaint about the Bhosari constituency with a list of 58 affected voters. He alleged that over 15,000 voters, some living in Bhosari for over 40 years, had their registrations shifted to distant areas such as Thane and Mumbai without their knowledge.

The alteration came to light after the October 2024 Maharashtra Assembly elections. Bogus ‘Form 8’ applications were submitted in the names of residents who had never relocated.

The Pune District Election Officer, who was ordered to hold the inquiry, has sought factual reports from election officers in eight constituencies: Bhosari, Pimpri, Kothrud, Khadakwasla, Khed Alandi, Ambegaon, Nashik East and Thane.

At Nigdi police station, Pimpri’s Assistant Electoral Registration Officer has lodged a non-cognizable offence against four suspects. They are accused of using four mobile phones to submit the Form 8 applications fraudulently.

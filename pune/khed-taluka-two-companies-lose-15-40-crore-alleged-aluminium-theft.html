Khed taluka: two companies lose over ₹15.40 crore in alleged aluminium theft

A case has been registered over an alleged aluminium theft from two Khed taluka companies, where the total estimated loss stands at ₹15,40,92,876.

Published 19 Nov 2026, 2:06 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Two companies in Khed taluka’s Vasuli industrial area have lost over ₹15.40 crore in an alleged aluminium theft that, according to the police complaint, took place between December 2018 and August 2026.

Two store officers working at the companies allegedly misused their positions and, in collusion with eight others, removed aluminium stock, scrap material and aluminium meant for recycling from the company premises.

The materials were allegedly taken outside the premises and sold to scrap dealers for financial gain. The investigation suggests the alleged involvement of contractual workers, a recruitment agency representative, transport workers and scrap dealers.

The total estimated loss to the two companies stands at ₹15,40,92,876. A case has been registered following the complaint.

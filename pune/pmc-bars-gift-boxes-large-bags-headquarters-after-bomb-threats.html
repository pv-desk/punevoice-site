PMC bars gift boxes, large bags at its headquarters after bomb threats

Different civic departments received bomb threats over the past month, and the Pune Municipal Corporation has now decided to keep gift boxes and large bags out of its headquarters for good.

Published 14 Nov 2026, 1:19 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

The Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) will permanently bar visitors who carry gift boxes or large bags from its headquarters, after different civic departments received bomb threats over the past month.

Police searched the civic headquarters on Thursday after another bomb threat came by email; nothing suspicious was found. Following the repeated threat emails, the civic body has decided to make the curbs permanent.

Visitors carrying gift boxes or articles packed in large bags will not be allowed in. The civic body had temporarily barred the distribution of gifts inside the main building during Diwali.

Additional Municipal Commissioner Prajit Nair said the decision was taken in view of the repeated threats and security concerns, as well as objections raised by social organisations.

The Pune Municipal Corporation is tightening security. Thousands of citizens visit the headquarters every day. Security arrangements are being strengthened, and officials will also watch for visitors behaving suspiciously.

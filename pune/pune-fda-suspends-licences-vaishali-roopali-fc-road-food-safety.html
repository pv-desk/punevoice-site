FDA suspends licences of popular Hotel Vaishali, Roopali Restaurant on FC Road over food safety violations

Hotel Vaishali and Roopali Restaurant on FC Road may not run any food business activity, home delivery, parcel services, takeaway and food sales included, until further orders.

Published 14 Nov 2026, 11:05 am IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Hotel Vaishali and Roopali Restaurant, two popular and well-known restaurants on Fergusson College (FC) Road, have had their food business licences suspended with immediate effect by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Maharashtra, after inspections on November 13, 2026 revealed serious violations of food safety and hygiene standards.

Assistant Commissioner (Food) Syed Imran Hashmi ordered the suspensions. The action was taken under the FDA’s Safe Food, Safe Maharashtra campaign.

Hotel Vaishali, at 1218, FC Road, Shivajinagar, scored 58 out of 98, which resulted in a ‘Non-Compliance’ classification. Officials said a freezer used for storing raw materials was placed immediately next to the women’s washroom and the griddle for dosa and uttappa was next to the men’s washroom. Officials said boiled potatoes, cooked rice and other prepared food were stored in an open corridor.

The restaurant had been inspected in June 2026 and issued a notice to rectify the deficiencies, but serious violations were found again.

Officials said the kitchen at Roopali Restaurant, at 1227/2, FC Road, Shivajinagar, was open and inadequately protected, exposing food preparation areas to dust, dirt and insects. They also found stagnant water on the premises, and food stored without proper covering, labelling or ‘Use By’ dates.

Both establishments are barred from any food business activity, including food sales, takeaway, parcel services and home delivery, until further orders. They can resume only after rectifying all major deficiencies, complying with food safety standards during a fresh inspection and obtaining written permission from the FDA.

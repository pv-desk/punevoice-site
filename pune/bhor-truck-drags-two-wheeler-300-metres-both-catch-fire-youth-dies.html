Bhor, Rambaug Road · Accidents and fires

In Bhor, truck drags two-wheeler for nearly 300 metres; both catch fire, youth dies

Both vehicles were completely gutted, and the driver is said to have fled.

Published 10 Nov 2026, 6:36 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

A speeding truck rammed into a two-wheeler on Rambaug Road in Bhor, Pune district, on Tuesday and dragged it for about 300 metres; both vehicles caught fire, and a young man died while another was seriously injured.

Preliminary information is that the accident took place near Navlai Temple, in front of the Bhor Sub-District Hospital.

Instead of stopping, the truck driver kept driving with the two-wheeler trapped underneath.

The friction from the dragging is said to have set off a fire that engulfed both vehicles, and the truck and the two-wheeler were completely gutted.

One of the riders died on the spot; the other suffered serious injuries. The truck driver is said to have abandoned the truck and fled. Further details are awaited.

Pune Camp: FDA suspends licence of Pasteur Ice Cream Parlour over serious hygiene violations

A Pune Camp ice cream parlour may not carry on any food-related business during the suspension of its food licence, the Food and Drug Administration has ordered.

Published 11 Nov 2026, 3:12 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Pasteur Ice Cream Parlour on MG Road, Pune Camp, had its food licence suspended with immediate effect on November 10 by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA), over serious hygiene violations.

The FDA took the action under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, as part of its ‘Safe Food Safe Maharashtra’ campaign. Food safety officer Satish Hake inspected the premises. Licensing authority N R Sarkate, designated officer of Circle 6, ordered the suspension until further orders.

According to the FDA, the kitchen had rodent droppings beneath racks and freezers, along with cobwebs, dust and peeling wall surfaces. Fruits and other food items were stored alongside sanitisers and disinfectants. Food products in freezers carried no best-before dates.

The parlour allegedly held only a retailer’s licence, yet it was preparing and selling samosas, sandwiches, burgers, pizzas, pav bhaji and falooda. It also failed to produce water and food testing reports and FoSTaC training certificates for its employees. A mandatory display about free drinking water was missing.

The FDA has prohibited any food-related business during the suspension. Joint Commissioner of FDA Pune division Shrikant Karkale warned that any violation of the order would invite strict legal action.

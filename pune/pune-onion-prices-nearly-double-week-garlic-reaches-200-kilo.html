Pune: onion prices nearly double in a week, garlic reaches ₹200 a kilo

Garlic has reached ₹200 a kilo in Pune, and onion prices there have nearly doubled in a week.

Published 14 Nov 2026, 1:45 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Onions in Pune are now selling at ₹45 to ₹50 a kilo, and their prices have nearly doubled in a week.

Garlic has reached ₹200 a kilo and is selling at ₹180 to ₹200 a kilo.

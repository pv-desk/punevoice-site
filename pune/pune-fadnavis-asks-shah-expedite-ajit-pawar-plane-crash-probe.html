Ajit Pawar crash probe · Politics

‘Accident or something more?’: Fadnavis asks Shah to expedite Ajit Pawar plane crash probe

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said Union Home Minister Amit Shah had assured him he would try to expedite the investigation into the plane crash that killed Ajit Pawar.

Published 16 Nov 2026, 3:25 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said people wanted to know whether the plane crash that killed former Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar was an accident or whether something more lay behind it, and urged Union Home Minister Amit Shah on Monday, 16 November, to expedite the probe. He was speaking at the unveiling of Ajit Pawar's bust at the Pune Zilla Parishad headquarters, and urged that the findings be made public at the earliest.

Fadnavis said he had personally requested Shah to intervene, and that Shah had assured him he would look into the matter and try to expedite the process.

“Nearly eight, nine, 10 months have passed, and a major investigation is underway with people and teams from America and various countries assisting in the probe. But the confusion in the minds of the common people of Maharashtra must be cleared,” Fadnavis said.

Shah unveiled the bust, and the main entrance gate of the Pune Zilla Parishad headquarters was named after Ajit Pawar.

Ajit Pawar died on 28 January 2026, when a Learjet 45 carrying him from Mumbai to Baramati crashed while attempting to land near Baramati airport. All five people aboard died.

The Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau's preliminary report of 28 February flagged poor visibility, inadequate navigational and weather facilities at the airport, faded runway markings and loose gravel, and did not establish any foul play. The final report is still awaited.

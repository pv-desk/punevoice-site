‘Nothing short of scam’: Bombay High Court bars Sinhgad trustees from institutions’ records

The Bombay High Court has directed that all documents of the Sinhgad Technical Education Society’s institutions be kept in the sealed custody of government-appointed administrators, who will take charge of managing the 32 institutions to which they have been appointed.

Published 20 Nov 2026, 4:03 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

A Bombay High Court division bench of Justice GS Kulkarni and Justice Neela Gokhale on Thursday barred trustees of the Sinhgad Technical Education Society (STES) from accessing or interfering with official records of its educational institutions.

The court directed that all documents be kept in the sealed custody of government-appointed administrators. The records will be made available to investigating agencies whenever required.

The bench gave the directions while hearing over 229 proceedings concerning alleged irregularities involving STES and its managing trustees. The court described the matter as “nothing short of a scam” and clarified that the CBI, the Enforcement Directorate and police are free to take action against the trustees in accordance with law.

The administrators, appointed to 25 technical and seven higher education institutions run by the society, will now take charge of managing the 32 institutions.

A trustee present in court was directed to file an affidavit by 11 am on 20 November, with no further extension, disclosing every bank account held by the society and its balance. The court was informed that two trustees were currently abroad, and sought their nationalities and passport details.

The bench directed administrators to ensure students’ education is not disrupted. Students may be shifted to other institutions, and institutions that cannot be operated may be closed, subject to the law and pending proceedings.

Concerned that academic activities at STES’s medical college may have remained disrupted for four to five weeks, the court made the National Medical Commission and the University of Health Sciences parties to the proceedings.

The matter is posted for further hearing at 11 am on 20 November.

Pimpri: MahaRERA orders Rose Crystal Developers to hand over Flat 805 with interest for delay

MahaRERA ordered Rose Crystal Developers to pay delayed-possession interest from April 1, 2024, until actual handover, and rejected a homebuyer's demand of ₹4,81,250 towards rental expenses.

Published 14 Nov 2026, 3:53 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

On November 5, 2026, MahaRERA Member-II Ravindra Deshpande ordered Rose Crystal Developers to hand over Flat No. 805 in B-Wing of its Rose Castle project in Borhadewadi, Pimpri, to the homebuyer, along with interest for the delay.

The developer must pay delayed-possession interest from April 1, 2024, until actual handover, at the rate prescribed under the Maharashtra RERA Rules.

The buyer approached MahaRERA after the developer allegedly failed to hand over the flat by the agreed deadline of March 31, 2024, despite receiving the full payment of ₹18,15,800. The buyer also raised concerns about incomplete internal work and common facilities.

The developer claimed it had received the Occupancy Certificate on January 31, 2025, had offered possession, and that the buyer had accepted the flat and was living there. It submitted a photograph of a nameplate outside the flat as proof.

MahaRERA rejected this, stating that a nameplate photograph does not prove actual possession. The developer failed to produce a possession letter, handover receipt or key-handover acknowledgement.

The developer's explanation that a shortage of construction materials caused the delay was also rejected for lack of supporting evidence.

MahaRERA also rejected the buyer's separate demand for ₹4,81,250 towards rental expenses and did not award additional compensation for mental agony.

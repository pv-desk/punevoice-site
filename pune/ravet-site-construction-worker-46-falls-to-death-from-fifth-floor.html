Pune: War veteran Lt Gen Ashok Joshi (retd), 90, dies after accidental fall

War veteran Lt Gen Ashok Joshi (retd), 90, died after an accidental fall in Pune on Wednesday morning. He allegedly fell from the terrace of his residential building in Salunke Vihar, Wanowrie.

Joshi, a decorated Army veteran and former Director General of Military Training (DGMT), participated in the 1965 and 1971 Indo-Pak wars.

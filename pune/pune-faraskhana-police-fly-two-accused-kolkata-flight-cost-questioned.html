Faraskhana police fly two accused from Kolkata to Pune; flight cost questioned

Faraskhana police flew two people accused in separate cases from Kolkata to Pune, and who decided on a flight when rail was available remains an open question.

Published 16 Nov 2026, 2:22 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Two accused who fled Pune for Kolkata after cases were registered were detained there and flown back to Pune by Faraskhana police.

The two are accused in separate cases that Faraskhana police were investigating. In the first case, 17 kg of silver jewellery and silver bars worth ₹40.90 lakh were stolen from a jeweller’s shop in Ganesh Peth. A craftsman at the shop was suspected. The shop owner checked CCTV footage, which showed him leaving the shop with a large bag around dawn.

In the second case, 1,850 grams of pure gold was taken from a Raviwar Peth jeweller on the pretext of making ornaments and was allegedly never returned, an embezzlement of about ₹2.59 crore. A case was registered against the accused and his manager.

On learning the two were in Kolkata, a team of one police officer and three personnel went in pursuit of them.

Questions are now being raised over the cost of the air travel, whether senior officers had given permission, and whether administrative approval was obtained. The investigation team is understood to have said it flew at the last minute because it missed its train.

Who decided on a flight when rail was available, how much it cost and whether it was paid from government funds remain open questions, as does whether senior police officers will order an inquiry into the expense.

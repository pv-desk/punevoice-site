JM Road Khau Galli: FDA orders 8 food establishments to stop operations, suspends 6 licences

The Food and Drug Administration, Pune, has proposed compounding penalties totalling Rs 2.40 lakh against eight unregistered food establishments in the JM Road Khau Galli area of Shivajinagar.

Published 19 Nov 2026, 12:11 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

The Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Pune, took action against 17 food establishments in the JM Road Khau Galli area of Shivajinagar in a special inspection drive on 18 November 2026.

Eight establishments were ordered to immediately stop operations for functioning without valid food safety registration or licences. The licences of six others were suspended with immediate effect for violating hygiene standards, and two more were served improvement notices.

Against each of the eight unregistered establishments, the FDA has proposed a compounding penalty of Rs 30,000, a total of Rs 2.40 lakh.

The drive was conducted by the FDA’s Pune Zone-2 team at JM Road Khau Galli, near the Junglee Maharaj Temple and around the NCR Hotel area. The action was taken under the FDA’s ‘Safe Food Safe Maharashtra’ campaign, for violations of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, and the rules framed under it.

The eight establishments ordered to stop operations are: Shrinath Pizza and Sandwiches (near NCR Hotel, JM Road), Raj Chinese Fast Food (JM Road), Hindavi Swarajya (opposite Junglee Maharaj Temple, JM Road), Everest Momos, Jai Malhar Bhurji Centre and Shawarma Centre (JM Road Khau Galli), and Utkarsh Mandal Love Juice Point and Sai Juice, Milkshake and Sharbat Centre (opposite Junglee Maharaj Temple, Ghole Road).

FDA Joint Commissioner (Pune Division) Shrikant Karkale said all establishments had been instructed to strictly comply with food safety regulations. Karkale warned that businesses should not resume operations until they fulfil the prescribed requirements.

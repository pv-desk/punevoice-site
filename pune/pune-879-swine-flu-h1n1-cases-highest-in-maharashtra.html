Swine flu · City

Pune reports 879 swine flu (H1N1) cases, highest in Maharashtra

Nagpur has reported the highest H1N1-related toll in Maharashtra, 12, with 35 deaths recorded across the state.

Published 13 Nov 2026, 1:00 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Pune has reported 879 swine flu (H1N1) cases, the most in Maharashtra; Mumbai has recorded 648 and Nagpur 493.

Maharashtra has recorded 35 H1N1-related deaths. Nagpur has reported the highest toll at 12, followed by Nashik (8), Pune (3) and Navi Mumbai (1).

H1N1 is an influenza virus originally associated with pigs. It spreads mainly from person to person through respiratory droplets and aerosols when an infected person coughs, sneezes or talks. Contact with pigs or eating properly cooked pork does not usually cause human infections.

To stay safe: wear a mask in crowded places, wash your hands frequently, cover your mouth and nose while coughing or sneezing, avoid close contact with infected people and stay home if you have flu symptoms. Get vaccinated as advised by a healthcare professional, avoid self-medication and consult a doctor, especially if symptoms are severe or you are at higher risk.

The figures are from health department data.

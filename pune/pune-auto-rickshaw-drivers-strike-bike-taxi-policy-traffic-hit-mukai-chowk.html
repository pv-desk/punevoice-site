Mukai Chowk · City

Pune auto-rickshaw drivers strike over bike taxi policy; traffic hit at Mukai Chowk

Auto-rickshaw drivers in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad want the Maharashtra Government to withdraw its bike taxi policy completely, fearing that bike taxi services will hurt their livelihood and employment.

Published 23 Nov 2026, 1:54 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Auto-rickshaw drivers in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad have called a strike on Monday, 23 November, and from 10 am protesting drivers gathered at Mukai Chowk in Pimpri-Chinchwad, disrupting traffic in the area.

The protesting drivers stopped bike taxis as well as Ola and Uber auto-rickshaws.

The protesters are demanding that the Maharashtra Government withdraw its bike taxi policy completely.

The drivers fear that bike taxi services will hurt their livelihood and employment.

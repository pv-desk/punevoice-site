Child-lifting rumour: two deaf-mute men tied up, beaten in Moshi

Two deaf-mute men tied up and beaten in Moshi on suspicion of kidnapping children had only come to a housing society to ask for donations, a police inquiry found.

Published 14 Nov 2026, 12:46 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

As per the police, local residents caught two deaf-mute men at Shivajiwadi in Moshi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, on suspicion of kidnapping children, a child-lifting rumour, and tied them up with rope; the men were also beaten.

On 13 November the Moshi marshals received a Dial 112 call saying locals had held two men at Shivajiwadi, within Moshi village limits, on suspicion of kidnapping children. Police rushed to the spot.

The police inquiry found that the two men had come to a housing society only to ask for donations, and no attempt to kidnap children, or any other wrongdoing, was found.

A non-cognisable offence has been registered against two people for beating the two men.

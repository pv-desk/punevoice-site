Supriya Sule: ‘Maharashtra deserves the truth’, await Ajit Pawar crash probe

NCP (SP) MP Supriya Sule has appealed for sensitivity from political parties and the media over a plane crash that killed former Deputy CM Ajit Pawar.

Published 17 Nov 2026, 4:37 pm IST · By Punevoice Desk

Share on WhatsApp

Speaking in Pune on Tuesday, NCP (SP) MP Supriya Sule said the Maharashtra Government must address the questions and doubts surrounding the plane crash that killed former Deputy CM Ajit Pawar, and urged people to await the probe.

She said Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis had demanded a transparent and time-bound inquiry before Union Home Minister Amit Shah, and that the truth must come out before the people of Maharashtra.

“Therefore, let us wait for the report,” she said.

On Rohit Pawar’s presentation and allegations about the crash, Sule said she had not watched his entire press conference as she had been in Delhi. She said she would speak to him to understand the details, including his demand for an FIR and an investigation into the role of the aircraft operator.

She said civil aviation is a Central subject and the competent authorities should take an appropriate decision.

“The person who died in this accident was the man who held our family together. My elder brother is gone,” she said, appealing to political parties and the media to approach the matter with sensitivity.

Sule also met the Pune District Collector over drought, water availability and traffic. She said farmers in the Baramati Lok Sabha constituency who have not received the ₹5,000 assistance should visit the Collector’s office and register their names.

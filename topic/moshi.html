Man and 12-year-old nephew drown in farm pond at Moshi

Two residents of Bankar Vasti in Moshi drowned in a farm pond at Moshi on Wednesday night: a 21-year-old man and his 12-year-old nephew.

They had gone swimming in a farm pond near their locality on Wednesday evening. Locals brought them out of the water and sent them to a hospital. Doctors there declared them dead.

Inspector Bhojraj Misal of Bhosari MIDC police said the two drowned during the swim. The police have registered a case of accidental death.

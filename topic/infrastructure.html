CCTV captures powerful blast at Trimurti Pharma in Kurkumbh MIDC

A powerful explosion at Trimurti Pharma in Kurkumbh MIDC, an industrial area in Pune district’s Daund taluka, was captured by CCTV cameras. Thick plumes of smoke spread across the company premises and into the surrounding area.

The cause of the blast remains unclear.

Officials have yet to confirm whether anyone was injured or killed, or how much damage the explosion caused.

Fire at Mulchand Sons godown in Pune’s Tulshibagh market, cause not yet known

A fire broke out at the Mulchand Sons godown in Pune’s Tulshibagh market, where Pune Municipal Corporation fire engines and firefighters were at the spot.

Shopkeepers and shoppers in the crowded area were alarmed.

Early indications point to a possible electrical short circuit. The cause, the status of the fire and any injuries are yet to be confirmed.

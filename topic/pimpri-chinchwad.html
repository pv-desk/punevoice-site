Pune East Postal Division approves independent PIN codes for Wakad, Punawale, Tathawade, Pimple Saudagar

The Pune East Postal Division of India Post has approved new independent PIN codes for the Pimpri-Chinchwad localities Wakad, Punawale, Tathawade and Pimple Saudagar.

Under the new arrangement, the codes are 411086 for Punawale, 411087 for Tathawade, 411088 for Wakad and 411083 for Pimple Saudagar.

The move is expected to streamline postal services, improve delivery accuracy and give the fast-growing localities a distinct identity.
